# Your art

Put a picture here (portrait pictures look best on a vertical monitor) and point the art scene at it
in `settings.ini`:

```ini
scene=art
art=art/my-picture.jpg
```

The scene slowly zooms the camera, drifts clouds and wisps across it, sends birds and motes through,
and at night darkens it with stars and fireflies. Pictures in this folder are ignored by git, so your
art stays on your machine.
